## 1. Basic Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Titration** | A technique used to determine the concentration of an unknown solution. |
| **Analyte** | The solution of unknown concentration. |
| **Titrant** | The solution of known concentration added during titration. |
| **Standard Solution** | A solution whose concentration is accurately known. |
| **Primary Standard** | A highly pure substance used to prepare a standard solution. |

---

## 2. Apparatus & Setup

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Burette** | A graduated tube used to deliver the titrant. |
| **Pipette** | Used to measure a fixed volume of the analyte. |
| **Conical Flask (Erlenmeyer flask)** | Holds the analyte during titration. |
| **Volumetric Flask** | Used to prepare standard solutions. |
| **Meniscus** | The curved surface of a liquid used for accurate reading. |

---

## 3. Reaction & Stoichiometry

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Neutralization Reaction** | Reaction between acid and base producing salt and water. |
| **Mole Ratio** | Ratio in which reactants combine (from balanced equation). |
| **Stoichiometry** | Calculation of quantities in chemical reactions. |
| **Molarity (M)** | Moles of solute per liter of solution. |
| **Concentration** | Amount of solute in a given volume. |

---

## 4. Key Points in Titration

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Equivalence Point** | The point where moles of titrant equal moles of analyte (stoichiometrically). |
| **End Point** | The point where the indicator changes color. |
| **Indicator** | A substance that changes color near the equivalence point. |
| **pH** | Measure of acidity or alkalinity. |
| **Titration Curve** | Graph of pH vs. volume of titrant added. |

---

## 5. Types of Titration

- **Acid–Base Titration** – Determines concentration of acid or base via neutralization.
- **Redox Titration** – Involves oxidation-reduction reactions.
- **Complexometric Titration** – Uses complex formation, typically with EDTA.
- **Precipitation Titration** – Forms a precipitate during the reaction.

---

## 6. Acid–Base Specific Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Strong Acid / Strong Base** | Completely dissociates in water. |
| **Weak Acid / Weak Base** | Partially dissociates in water. |
| **pKa / pKb** | Acid/base dissociation constants; measures strength. |
| **Buffer Solution** | Resists changes in pH when small amounts of acid/base are added. |
| **Half-equivalence Point** | Point where half the analyte is neutralized; pH = pKa for weak acids. |

---

## 7. Measurement Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Titre Value (Titer)** | Volume of titrant used to reach the endpoint. |
| **Initial Reading** | Starting burette reading before titration begins. |
| **Final Reading** | Ending burette reading after reaching endpoint. |
| **Concordant Results** | Titration values that closely agree (usually within 0.1 cm³). |

---

## 8. Accuracy & Errors

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Systematic Error** | Consistent, reproducible error caused by faulty equipment or method. |
| **Random Error** | Unpredictable variation due to environmental or human factors. |
| **Parallax Error** | Misreading due to viewing angle of the meniscus. |
| **Standardization** | Process of determining the exact concentration of a solution. |

---

## 9. Indicators (Common Examples)

| Indicator | Color Change | pH Range |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| **Phenolphthalein** | Colorless → Pink | 8.2 – 10.0 |
| **Methyl Orange** | Red → Yellow | 3.1 – 4.4 |
| **Bromothymol Blue** | Yellow → Blue | 6.0 – 7.6 |
